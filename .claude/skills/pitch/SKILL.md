Read `docs/PROBLEM.md`. If it does not exist, stop and tell the team to
run `/grill-with-docs` first — a pitch without a validated problem is
just a guess said confidently.

## Write the pitch

Three minutes spoken is roughly 400 words. Five beats, in this order:

1. **The person** (~45s) — who, specifically, and the moment you watched
   them struggle. Open with the real scene, not a statistic.
2. **Today** (~30s) — the workaround, and what it costs them.
3. **Our fix** (~45s) — the MVP in one sentence, then what the person
   will actually do with it.
4. **The demo moment** (~30s) — what you will show working in Session 3.
5. **How we'll know** (~30s) — the evidence you will bring: who tried it
   and what happened.

Write it to be spoken aloud: short sentences, no bullet points, no
jargon, nothing the team could not say naturally. If the team has more
than one presenter, mark who speaks each beat.

## Pressure-test it

List the three hardest questions a sceptical facilitator will ask, each
with a suggested honest answer. At least one must attack the weakest
point in `docs/PROBLEM.md`. If the problem evidence is thin, say so
directly and suggest how to strengthen it before they present.

Write both to `docs/PITCH.md`. Count the words: if the script runs over
450, cut it.
